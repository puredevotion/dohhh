Cow-meat. Chinese does not have a separate word for beef the way English does — it names the animal and the substance, and lets you do the arithmetic.

One character learned, four dishes readable. 牛 turns up again in 牛奶 milk and 牛排 steak.

肉 TRAVELS IN DISGUISE

Squeezed into the left of another character, 肉 flattens into 月. Once you see it, the organ section of a menu stops being a lottery:

肝liver 肠intestine 肚tripe 腰kidney 脑brain

A hint, not a rule — the same 月 is also the moon, as in 月票 monthly pass. But on a menu it is nearly always flesh, and that is the one place you need it.
